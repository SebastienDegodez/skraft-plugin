# Milestone M1 — Checkout pricing

## Story: loyalty-discount

As a returning customer
I want my loyalty tier reflected in the checkout total
So that the price I see rewards my history with the brand

### Business rules under discussion

- Bronze, Silver and Gold customers do not currently receive anything different.
- Finance wants the discount expressed in whole cents, never fractional.
- Legal has not yet confirmed whether the discount stacks with promotional codes.

### Status

Nothing has been designed or built for this story. No architecture decision has been
taken, no acceptance criteria have been agreed, and no code has been written.
