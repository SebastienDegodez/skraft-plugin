# Milestone M1 — Checkout pricing

## Story: discount

As a returning customer
I want my loyalty tier, my promo code and my shipping choice reflected in the checkout quote
So that the price I see rewards my history with the brand

### Scope

- Loyalty discount by tier, its cap and its minimum subtotal; Platinum joins the tiers.
- The WELCOME5 and SUMMER10 promo codes; any other code is ignored.
- Standard and express shipping fees, and the checkout quote that adds them.
- Negative subtotals, persistence and HTTP transport are out of scope.

### Status

Researched. The acceptance criteria are agreed and the research is approved; nothing is designed or built yet.
