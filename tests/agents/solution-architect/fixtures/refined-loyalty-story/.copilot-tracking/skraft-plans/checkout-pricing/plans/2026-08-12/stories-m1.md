# Milestone M1 — Checkout pricing

## Story: loyalty-discount

As a returning customer
I want my loyalty tier reflected in the checkout total
So that the price I see rewards my history with the brand

### Refined scope

- Bronze, Silver and Gold each earn a different reduction on the basket subtotal.
- The reduction is always a whole number of cents.
- Promotional codes are out of scope for this milestone.

### Status

Refined and ready for design. RESEARCH is complete; nothing has been designed or built.
