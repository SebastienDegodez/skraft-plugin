# Milestone M1 — Checkout pricing

## Story: loyalty-discount

As a returning customer
I want my loyalty tier reflected in the checkout total
So that the price I see rewards my history with the brand

### Scope

- Bronze, Silver and Gold are the tiers in production today.
- A Platinum tier is arriving next quarter. Its reduction has **not** been decided by the
  business and is not part of this milestone.
- Promotional codes are out of scope.

### Status

Designed. The design pass is complete and the decisions are ratified.
